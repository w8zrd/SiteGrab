Runs a mirroring crawl of the site you give it and packages everything into a zip. Large or slow sites can take a few minutes. Only fetches public pages — nothing behind a login, and it respects each site's robots.txt.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads. Only archive sites you own or have permission to copy — see terms.
